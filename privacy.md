# Privacy policy - rclone-agentic

Last updated: 14 November 2026

rclone-agentic is a private tool used only by its owner to synchronise the owner's own Google Drive folder with the owner's own iCloud Drive.

- **Data accessed:** only files in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, with the owner's explicit consent.
- **Use of data:** files are copied between the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's own iCloud Drive on the owner's own computer. Nothing else is done with them.
- **Sharing:** no data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
- **Storage:** the access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- **Other users:** the tool is not offered to anyone else.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
